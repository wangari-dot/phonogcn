SYNTHETIC DATA NOTICE
=====================

This dataset is a fully synthetic placeholder for the KSL-Daily-500
benchmark described in the PhoneGCN paper.

PURPOSE
-------
It exists to:
  1. Allow reviewers and users to run the full PhoneGCN training and
     evaluation pipeline end-to-end without real data.
  2. Verify the data schema (manifests, skeleton arrays, crop paths)
     that the pipeline expects.
  3. Serve as a structural template for constructing the real dataset
     from KBC/Citizen TV footage under a Research Data Use Agreement.

WHAT IS SYNTHETIC
-----------------
  - Skeleton sequences: procedurally generated smooth trajectories through
    signing space, informed by KSL phonological location categories
    (Mweri 2018). They resemble signing motion but are NOT real KSL.
  - Hand/face features: random unit vectors (NOT real image crops).
  - Text: Swahili sentence templates with substituted tokens.
  - Gloss-to-clip assignment: procedural (random), NOT annotated signing.

WHAT IS REAL
------------
  - The gloss vocabulary (gloss_vocabulary.json) is the 607-gloss
    expert-validated KSL vocabulary (source: cleaned_expert_validated.csv,
    validated by KSL-fluent reviewers). The labels are real; their
    assignment to these synthetic clips is not.

REAL DATASET ACCESS
-------------------
  The real KSL-Daily-500 dataset is withheld under the Kenya Data
  Protection Act 2019. Contact the corresponding author for access
  under a Research Data Use Agreement.
